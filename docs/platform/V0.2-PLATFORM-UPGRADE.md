# SDI One Platform Upgrade v0.2

## Purpose

Move SDI One from an operational explorer toward a platform by adding permanent homes for the engines that future development will activate.

## Platform Engines

- Entity Engine: imported business records become SDI One objects.
- Workspace Engine: every object receives overview, analytics, relationships, timeline, documents and AI context.
- Relationship Engine: connects customers, jobs, invoices, invoice lines, vendors, equipment and inventory.
- Timeline Engine: one chronological activity layer across systems.
- Document Engine: shared attachments and files for all workspaces.
- Notification Engine: exception and alert framework.
- Security Engine: role + company + job permission model.
- Integration Engine: connector workspaces for every SDI source system.

## Development Principle

Keep the working import/data foundation. Add platform services incrementally around it.
